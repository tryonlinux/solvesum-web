How to play

Swap tiles until a row or column adds up to its target.

→
7−2 945 0−33
  1. Each row has a target on the left and each column a target on top. Adding up the lines is up to you.
  2. Tap a tile, then tap a tile next to it (left, right, up or down) to swap them. You can also swipe a tile toward its neighbour.
  3. When a line's sum matches its target, it's solved: you score, its tiles are replaced with new numbers and it gets a new target.
  4. Each line is worth 10. Solve more than one with a single swap for a combo: 2 lines score ×2 each, 3 lines ×3 each, and so on. New tiles can set off a chain, which adds to the combo.
  5. Every swap costs one from your supply. The round ends when you run out.
  6. Hint spends 2 swaps to plan a full route to a solved line. The tiles to swap glow one pair at a time, and the target line is outlined. Follow the plan to the end at no extra cost; any other swap cancels it. Shuffle deals a new board, free, 3 times per round.

Every target can be reached from the board it was dealt with. The Daily board is the same for everyone, and your first finish is the one that counts. Tile colours show sign and size: green is positive, red is negative.
